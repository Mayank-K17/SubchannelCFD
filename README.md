## Introduction
Subchannel CFD is a CFD-based Subchannel analysis tool. This tool is designed to perform thermal hydraulics analyses of full-scale engineering geometry of reactor systems (e.g. a full nuclear reactor core), while maintaining reasonable computational cost. Remarkably, it has been demonstrated that SubChCFD is 2 to 3 orders of magnitude less computationally expensive than traditional CFD tools. This significant reduction in computational cost enables routine engineering design calculations and safety analyses of nuclear reactors. With years of development, SubChCFD can be applied to various types of fission reactors, including Pressurised Water Reactor (PWR), Advanced Gas-cooled Reactor (AGR), Super-critical Water Reactor (SCWR) and prismatic High Temperature Gas-cooled Reactor (HTGR).

SubChCFD is built on EDF’s open-source multi-purpose CFD software [Code_Saturne](https://github.com/code-saturne/code_saturne). To use SubChCFD, the same version of [Code_Saturne](https://github.com/code-saturne/code_saturne) should be installed as prerequisite.
